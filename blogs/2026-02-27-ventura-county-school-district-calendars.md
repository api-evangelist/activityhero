Ventura County school calendar and school holidays. Ventura County Public Schools have holidays for Labor Day, Indigenous People's Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Recess, Winter Break, MLK Jr Day, Lunar New Year, Presidents Day, Spring Break, Cesar Chavez Day and Memorial Day. Summer Break is over 10 weeks long for Ventura County School District.
